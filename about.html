Clinical Curator & AI Strategist

Dr. Sergey Morozov

Physician executive, published researcher, and AI governance architect with two decades at the intersection of medicine and intelligent systems.

As a Clinical Curator and AI Strategist, Dr. Sergey Morozov brings the unique perspective of a physician executive who has overseen the deployment of AI across massive healthcare networks. Based in Brussels at the heart of European health policy, he operates at the intersection of clinical medicine, regulatory frameworks, and intelligent systems design.

With oversight of over 1.3 million diagnostic exams and leadership roles in networks of 100+ diagnostic centers, he understands that AI is not a software purchase—it is a clinical process transformation that must be managed with the same rigor as any medical intervention.

His methodology, Clinical Curation, ensures that technology serves the physician, the patient, and the institution — delivering clinical integrity, operational efficiency, and executive accountability in equal measure.

As Past President of the European Society of Medical Imaging Informatics (EuSoMII) and author of a textbook with over 400,000 downloads, Dr. Morozov has shaped the academic and policy foundations of AI in radiology across Europe and beyond.
