02 / 05 Why & what — pilots already done

The problem we close

  • Ambiguous conclusions & missing follow-up — a source of diagnostic error and litigation
  • Manual QA doesn't scale to thousands of trauma reports
  • BoneView's value depends on running on the right exams and agreeing with the report

Hypothesis: flag issues at full volume with agreement close to expert-to-expert — continuous QA in a human-in-the-loop mode.

03 / 05 Approach

Two tracks by the nature of each dimension

Hard dimensions · Q2, Q3, B1, B2

Fine-tuned French encoder + rules

CamemBERT-bio / DrBERT — ready open-source French medical models. Well-defined classification & slot extraction, bootstrapped from the pilot labels. Cheap at inference.

Soft dimension · Q1 ambiguity

LLM-as-judge, rubric-anchored

Open-weights LLM (Mistral / Qwen) scores against explicit signals — definite result? hedge resolved by an action? — not a free-form "rate 1–5". Reproducible.

BoneView intended use — the yardstick for B1 / B2

Conventional radiographs of limbs, pelvis, thoracic & lumbar spine, rib cage · patients ≥ 2 years · detecting fractures, dislocations, effusions, bone lesions. Out of scope: skull, face, cervical spine, chest/abdomen soft-tissue, CT/MRI/US. Coded as auditable rules, not vibes.

Privacy: open-weights, on-prem, de-identified (patient data · RGPD/GDPR). Nothing trained from scratch.

04 / 05 3-month roadmap

Pilots done → consolidate, model, ship

Both pilots already produced labeled data, so the plan starts from consolidation — not from scratch. Model ceiling is bounded by inter-annotator agreement, measured first.
